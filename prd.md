Hardwood Flooring FAQ
Is hardwood suitable for every room?
Not necessarily. Room conditions, moisture exposure, subfloor conditions, and intended use should be considered before selecting hardwood.
How long does hardwood installation take?
The timeline depends on the size and condition of the space, flooring type, preparation requirements, and installation method.
Can hardwood replace existing flooring?
In many projects, existing flooring can be removed and replaced, subject to site conditions.
